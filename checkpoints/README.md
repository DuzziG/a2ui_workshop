# Checkpoints

Each folder holds the complete `lib/` folder of the app at the **end** of that step of the [workshop](../README.md).

Fell behind? Copy a checkpoint's `lib/` over your own. Your `lib/api_key.dart` isn't in the checkpoints, so copying never overwrites your key.

The checkpoints build the example app, Fridge Chef. Once you've caught up, put your own idea back in: your persona and your components.

| Folder | The app after this step |
| ------ | ----------------------- |
| `step_01` | App shell, chatting with Gemini in plain text |
| `step_02` | A hand-written recipe card with data binding and an action |
| `step_03` | Gemini writes the UI, with a persona and the X-ray view |
| `step_04` | Photos, and actions go back to Gemini |
| `step_05` | Your own component: a cooking timer |
| `step_06` | Streaming, and A2UI Express |
| `side_quest_a` | Step 6 plus functions Gemini calls |
| `side_quest_b` | Step 6 plus AI-painted pictures |
| `side_quest_c` | Step 6 plus a 3D companion (needs `flutter pub add flutter_scene vector_math`) |

All the other packages are added in Step 1.
